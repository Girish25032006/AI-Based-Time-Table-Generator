Timetable Constraints

Configure the rules used by the timetable generation system.
Admin
Constraint Context
1. Working Days

Select the days on which classes can be scheduled.

2. Periods / Time Slots

Define the daily class periods.

3. Lunch / Break
4. Faculty Daily Limit

Maximum teaching hours a faculty member can receive in one day.

5. Semester / Student Daily Limit

Maximum class hours for a semester/batch in one day.

6. Lab Consecutive Periods

Lab classes must use consecutive periods.

7. Clash Prevention
A faculty member cannot teach two classes at the same time.
A semester cannot have two subjects at the same time.
8. P-Cycle / C-Cycle

Semester 1 and 2 are common semesters. The timetable must respect the selected P-Cycle or C-Cycle.

9. Subject Theory / Lab Rules

These values are automatically taken from the Subject and Faculty Subject Assignment modules.

  • Theory hours come from subject data.
  • Lab hours come from subject data.
  • Theory + Lab must follow the assigned Theory and Co-Lab faculty.
  • Lab periods must be consecutive.
  • Faculty workload comes from the Faculty Subject Assignment module.
Save Timetable Constraints
These constraints will be used during timetable generation.
